ShiftWake Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

ShiftWake helps shift workers schedule wake-up alarms around their shifts. We keep your data private: the current version does not collect any personal data.

We do not collect personal data

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No ads or cross-app tracking.
  • No third-party analytics or crash reporting tools.
  • Your schedule, calendars, alarms, departure records, and settings are not uploaded to developer servers. ShiftWake has no servers of its own.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data.

What is stored on your device

To provide its features, ShiftWake stores the following on your device:

  • Shifts you create or import, shift templates, and date statuses such as days off and leave;
  • Settings such as wake-up offsets, repeat alarm counts and intervals, and departure reminders;
  • Scheduled alarms, alarm check results, and the last 30 calendar imports that contained changes;
  • Actual departure times when departure reminders are enabled, used for monthly summaries and automatically deleted after about 400 days.

This information stays on your device. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and Siri read the necessary shift and alarm times locally through the system’s App Group feature, without using the network. Deleting the app also deletes this data.

Calendar access is optional

ShiftWake reads your selected calendar only when you choose Import from Calendar and grant access. Future events are used to create shifts and alarms. Past events are shown only as history in the calendar and monthly summary. ShiftWake does not change or delete calendar events or upload calendar contents.

Alarms, notifications, and background refresh

  • Alarm access is used to create wake-up and departure alarms. It is required for the core alarm feature.
  • Optional notification access is used for shift changes, bedtime checks, and reminders before departure. It is not used for advertising or marketing.
  • Background refresh occasionally checks shift and alarm status when allowed by the system. It does not connect to the internet or upload data.

Siri and Shortcuts

You can ask Siri for your next wake-up time or use Shortcuts to add shifts and confirm departure. These actions read and write ShiftWake data only on your device. ShiftWake does not read NFC stickers itself. You set up sticker-triggered automations in the Shortcuts app.

Files you choose to export or share

  • Backups: you can export your schedule and settings to a file and save it wherever you choose, including iCloud Drive. Backups may contain shift names and source calendar names.
  • Schedule and summary images: these contain dates, shift times, and summary figures. You choose who receives them.
  • Diagnostic reports: these include check times, permission status, alarm counts, and error codes. They do not contain full shift or calendar names and are never sent automatically. They may contain information about your daily routine, so review them before sending.

You are responsible for these files after they leave ShiftWake.

Contacting the developer

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments. We use them only to reply and help resolve your issue. Please do not send passwords or other unnecessary information.

Children

ShiftWake is intended for adult workers and does not knowingly collect information about children. It does not collect personal information about anyone.

Alarm limitations

ShiftWake makes every effort to create system alarms. However, a powered-off device, permission changes, system limits, system errors, or accidental actions may affect reminders. Check your alarms before bed for important shifts and keep a backup alarm if needed.

Policy changes

Before adding accounts, cloud sync, analytics, or other network features, we will update this policy and the app’s App Store privacy information.
