ShiftWake Support

ShiftWake schedules wake-up alarms from your shift schedule. It supports iPhone and iPad running iOS 26 or later.

Contact the developer

Support email: tangpeng1996@outlook.com

Please describe the problem and include your device model, iOS version, and ShiftWake version. You can open an email from Settings › Help › Contact developer in the app, or write to the address above directly. Please do not send passwords or other unnecessary personal information.

Getting started

  1. In Schedule, tap + in the top-right corner to add a shift, schedule in bulk (by weekday, by rotation, or copy last week), or import from Calendar.
  2. If needed, adjust your wake-up offset, number of alarms, and alarm interval in Settings › Wake-up alarms.
  3. Check your next wake-up time and alarm status in Today.

ShiftWake needs alarm access to create system alarms. Calendar import and notifications are optional.

When alarm status needs attention

Read the notice in Today, then open Alarm Check to check again or follow the instructions on the page. In Alarm Check, use Run a test to schedule a test alarm (also available from Settings › Help › Alarm check and test). Confirm on a real device that you hear it ring.

A powered-off device, permission changes, system limits, or system errors can affect reminders. Check your alarms before bed for important shifts and keep a backup alarm if needed.

Stopping remaining alarms after you wake up

In Today, tap I’m awake to stop the remaining alarms for the current shift. After you edit or delete a shift or mark it as leave, check the alarm update result.

Calendar import

Choose a source calendar and grant access in Settings › Calendar import (or Schedule › + › Import from Calendar). Shifts are imported to your device, and changes from the source calendar and sync results are shown in the app. Past shifts in the calendar view are for viewing, summaries, and sharing only. No alarms are created for them.

You can mark shifts from Calendar as leave in ShiftWake. To delete the source event, use the Calendar app. If you change a shift’s time in ShiftWake, your local time is kept. The edit screen shows the most recently read calendar time, and you can tap Follow Calendar again, then save.

Backup, restore, and deleting data

Export or restore your schedule in Settings › Backup and restore. Backups may contain shift names and source calendar information, so keep them safe. Restoring merges shifts from the backup and reschedules alarms on the current device. Duplicate shifts are skipped. Shifts you have already changed, or that overlap existing shifts, are not restored by default; you can choose to add them one by one in the preview.

If the app says your schedule can’t be read, existing alarms are kept and automatic updates pause. Restore from a backup you exported earlier, or contact the developer if you can’t restore.

ShiftWake needs no account and does not upload your schedule. Deleting the app usually deletes its data on your device. Files you exported must be deleted separately where you saved them. Before deleting or stopping use of the app, check the alarms already in the system.

Diagnostics and privacy

You can export a diagnostic report from Alarm Check. It includes check times, status, error codes, and counts. It does not contain full shift or calendar names and is never sent to the developer automatically. Review it before sending.

The full privacy policy is available in Settings › Privacy policy. The public page and the app use the same policy.
